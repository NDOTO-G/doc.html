# Publication evidence

The authoritative public evidence record is [`evidence.doc.html`](evidence.doc.html). It includes positive, negative, null, and qualified results, and is itself a conforming manifest-first `doc.html`.

`SPEC.md` remains normative. Full laboratory populations, transcripts, private pull requests, seals, and corpora are retained in the private upstream repository and are not part of the public bundle. The evidence artifact names those upstream records as provenance identifiers without presenting them as public links.
