About this document

This is a writing-room-tail doc.html whose articles carry both witness epochs (§6.7): turns 1–2 are consecrated (SHA-256, sealed); turns 3–4 are writing-room (live UTC-timestamp).

A conforming reader classifies each witness by form alone and reports the scoped verdict PASS (verified=m, ordinal=k) — verified counts recomputed consecrated bytes, ordinal counts grammar-valid writing-room witnesses. The two counts are asserted separately, never blended into one number.

What is a mixed-epoch tail?

A tail where the earlier turns are consecrated (SHA-256, sealed for all time) and the later, still-live turns carry writing-room timestamps. Both registers are verified: consecrated turns recompute, writing-room turns are grammar-and-order valid.
